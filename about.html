The founder

An AI/ML engineer with a wider creative practice

Interest in artificial intelligence and machine learning opened the door to a bigger question: how can technology help an idea become useful in the real world?

That curiosity grew into work across website and software development, visual design, motion, video editing and digital marketing. Teal Stack Studios brings those skills together for clients who want to make their business easier to find, understand and use online.

The studio works with startups, small businesses and individual clients. Projects begin with the business goal and move into a scope that fits the need.
