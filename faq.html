Frequently asked questions

Answers about our services, project process and pricing. If you need help with something else, contact us at tealstackstudios@gmail.com.

What services does Teal Stack Studios offer?

We work across website development, custom software, digital marketing, graphic design and video editing. You can choose one service or combine them around the needs of your project.

How do I get a quote for my project?

Tell us what you want to achieve through the appointment form. We’ll discuss the goal, agree on the scope and deliverables, then share a proposal with the project price before work begins.

Are the prices on the website final?

No. Listed amounts are starting prices. The final quote depends on the agreed scope, including the features, content, integrations and deliverables involved. Website maintenance, when needed, is scoped and priced separately.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the project’s scope and requirements. We’ll outline an expected schedule in the proposal once we understand the work and what materials or feedback are needed from you.

Can I request changes during the project?

Yes. We’ll agree on deliverables and review expectations before the project starts. If the brief changes or new work is added, we’ll discuss any effect on scope, timing and price with you first.

Do you provide support after a website launches?

Post-launch support and maintenance can be discussed as a separate service. Any ongoing work and monthly charges are agreed separately from the initial website build.

Who do you work with?

We work with startups, small businesses, independent founders and clients who need practical digital work shaped around a clear business goal.
