Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Information you send

If you use the inquiry form, Teal Stack Studios receives the name, email address, service selection, preferred appointment details and project description you provide. The information is used to respond to your request and discuss possible work.

Form processing

The inquiry form is configured for Netlify Forms when this site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify processes the submission for the studio. The studio must enable an email notification in its hosting settings for inquiries to be forwarded to tealstackstudios@gmail.com.

Fonts and external links

This site requests the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google to retrieve the font. The Instagram link opens Instagram only when you choose to follow it; this site does not embed an Instagram feed.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not intentionally use analytics or advertising tracking cookies, and it does not embed third-party social media content. Hosting or form services may use technical storage needed to deliver their service. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated.

Retention and requests

Inquiry details should be kept only as long as needed to respond and manage the resulting business conversation, or as required by applicable law. To ask about or request deletion of an inquiry, email tealstackstudios@gmail.com.

Contact

For questions about this notice or information you submitted, contact tealstackstudios@gmail.com.
