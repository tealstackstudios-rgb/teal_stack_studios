Start with the collection

Mahitha Gold & Diamonds needed a digital presence that could carry the richness of South Indian craftsmanship while making a broad jewellery collection easier to explore.

Bridal trousseaus, temple heritage pieces, everyday designs and custom engagement jewellery each needed room to tell their story, alongside a practical way for the team to manage the catalogue.

Make room to browse

The information architecture follows the way customers browse jewellery. The responsive storefront gives the collections clear paths and lets visitors send consultation enquiries.

The project also includes a Python and Node.js backend, an SQLite database and an admin panel so the catalogue can be updated without editing the website by hand.

A catalogue the team can update

The result is a responsive jewellery website with collection browsing, custom enquiry paths and catalogue management in one place.
