Give the footage a through-line

Raw footage and static brand assets need thoughtful pacing and a visual through-line to become a story people want to watch.

The work is to make each edit feel clear, polished and recognizably connected to the brand across digital formats.

Build the edit around the message

The process begins with the message, audience and intended platform. Footage is shaped into a purposeful edit, then extended with kinetic typography, lower thirds, 2D and 3D animation, and custom effects.

After Effects supports motion and compositing, Photoshop prepares visual assets, and CapCut supports flexible editing and delivery.

Make the message easier to watch

The outcome is a platform-ready brand film combining edited footage, custom motion graphics and a consistent visual identity.
