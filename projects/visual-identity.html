Keep the identity consistent

A brand needs more than attractive graphics. Its identity has to stay clear and consistent wherever people encounter it.

The challenge is balancing a distinct visual point of view with the practical needs of campaigns, digital content and print.

Design for where it appears

The visual direction starts with the brand objective, audience and placement for each asset. Color, typography, imagery and layout become a system that can be adapted across digital assets and marketing materials.

Photoshop, Figma and Canva are used to create, refine and prepare work for screen and print.

A coordinated set of assets

The result is a set of visual materials prepared for their intended channels, with layouts and exports ready for digital and print use.
